# Service Management and SRE Teams

These readings cover the service lifecycle, from gathering requirements and planning resilient systems to launching services, managing conversions, recovering from disasters, and supporting customers. The SRE collection develops team training, interrupt management, recovery from operational overload, collaboration, and service engagement, followed by lessons from other industries and practical reference material.
